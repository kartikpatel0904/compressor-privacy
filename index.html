Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Compressor - PDF & Image ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Our app processes all files locally on your device. We do not:

  • Upload your files to any server
  • Access your files outside of the compression process
  • Store copies of your documents or images
  • Collect personal information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts or calendar

File Access

The app requires permission to access files on your device solely to:

  • Read the PDF or image files you select for compression
  • Save the compressed files to your chosen location

Files are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted over the internet.

Advertising

Our app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information to serve personalized ads, including:

  • Device advertising identifier
  • Device information (model, operating system)
  • IP address (for general location-based ads)
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings under Google Ads settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

Since all file processing occurs locally on your device, your documents and images remain under your control at all times. We have no access to your files.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app or on this page with an updated revision date.
